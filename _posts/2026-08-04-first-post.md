## Hey all,

Welcome to my blog, where I'll talk about things I've learned and/or think are cool. I intend for this blog to primarily be a place for me to practice somewhat casual somewhat academic long-form writing. 

In terms of a life update, I'm currently a rising third year at UC Irvine, and I'm working with the UCI NLP group. Right now, my idea that I'm working towards is developing an effective system to couple dense token-level signals with sparse environment rewards to improve on current RL paradigms. I think there's definitely still work to be done here to squeeze the maximum potential out of RLVR. I'll eventually get to a full length post about all the things I've learned about this topic.

Otherwise, I'm trying to self-learn CUDA programming and just general parallel programming techniques, since on-device AI is something I'm very interested in. Alongside this, I'm really interested in intelligent autonomous systems, i.e. systems built with reinforcement learning/unsupervised learning methods. I've recently been very interested in autonomous vehicles, partially because I think driving as a necessity for commutes, etc. is a problem that can be solved. Also I hate sitting in traffic.

I'm looking forward to learning more as always, and am now excited to share with any readers of this blog (including future me). Always feel free to hit me up at my socials in the footer, I'd love to talk more about anything on here.
